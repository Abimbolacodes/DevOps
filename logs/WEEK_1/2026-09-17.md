### What I learned

- Why companies test DSA in interviews — it shows efficient problem-solving, not just "does it work"
- The one-pass approach to Best Time to Buy/Sell Stock: track the lowest price seen so far and the best profit possible so far, instead of checking every pair

### Code I studied
```python
def max_profit(prices):
    min_price = float('inf')
    max_profit = 0
    for price in prices:
        if price < min_price:
            min_price = price
        elif price - min_price > max_profit:
            max_profit = price - min_price
    return max_profit
```
